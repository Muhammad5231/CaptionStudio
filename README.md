# 🎬 CaptionStudio Pro – Desktop Edition V4

**Professional video captioning suite with intelligent layout, real‑time preview, and cinematic word animations.**

---

## 📌 Description

CaptionStudio Pro is a powerful desktop application for creating stunning, animated captions for videos.  
It goes far beyond simple subtitles – it gives you full control over typography, outlines, shadows, glows, active word highlighting, and kinetic animations.  
The built‑in **Smart Layout Engine** automatically arranges words into clean, screen‑safe lines, and the **Live Preview** lets you see your design in motion instantly.

Whether you're creating content for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, or professional presentations, CaptionStudio Pro helps you produce captions that look like they were made by a Hollywood motion graphics studio.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Accurate Word‑Level Timing** – Parses SRT files and assigns each word its own start/end time for frame‑perfect synchronization.
- **Smart Caption Grouping Engine** – Automatically breaks long text into balanced lines, respecting screen‑safe margins and your preferred words‑per‑line.
- **30+ Built‑in Premium Styles** – One‑click presets like *Alex Hormozi*, *MrBeast*, *Netflix*, *Cyberpunk*, *Gaming RGB*, and many more.
- **Advanced Typography** – Choose any system font, adjust size, bold/italic, letter spacing, case mode (uppercase, lowercase, etc.), and color.
- **Multi‑Layer Effects** – Apply outlines (single or double), soft shadows, radiance glow, and text‑style effects (Gold Foil, Fire, Ice, Cyberpunk, RGB, Metal, etc.).
- **Active Word Highlighting** – The currently spoken word gets a dynamic highlight – choose from background boxes, rounded rectangles, underline, color change, scale & color, or glow.
- **Kinetic Word Animations** – Animate each word with entry, loop, and exit animations (Pop, Bounce, Elastic, Scale, Rotate, Drop, Shake, Wave, Pulse, and more).
- **Real‑Time Preview** – See your captions animate live as you adjust settings.
- **Template System** – Save and load your complete workspace as `.cstemplate` files. Browse, favorite, and share templates using the built‑in Template Library.
- **High‑Quality Video Export** – Renders directly to MP4 using FFmpeg (with customizable codec, CRF, preset).
- **Dark Professional UI** – Designed for comfort during long editing sessions.
